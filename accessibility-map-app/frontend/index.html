⚡ Features

Built for Inclusion

A platform designed with and for differently-abled citizens of India.

🗺️

Interactive Accessibility Map

Real-time map with color-coded markers showing fully, partially, or inaccessible locations across 28 Indian cities.

✅

Community Verification

5-verification system ensures data accuracy. Upvote, downvote, and confirm accessibility claims with photo evidence.

📊

Accessibility Score

10-point scoring across ramps, elevators, toilet access, door width, braille signage, and staff assistance.

📸

Photo Evidence Upload

Upload photos and short videos to prove accessibility claims. Builds trust and helps users make informed decisions.

🏆

City Leaderboard

Track which Indian cities lead in accessibility. Gamified civic participation drives improvement nationwide.

🤖

AI Accessibility Analysis

Claude AI analyzes uploaded photos to auto-detect accessibility features and suggest improvements.

🇮🇳 Vikshit Bharat 2047

Pillar 6: Social Inclusion & Justice

India's vision of becoming a developed nation by 2047 requires that progress reaches every citizen. Under Nari Shakti and Sugamya Bharat, we commit to an India where no one is left behind— where differently-abled, elderly, and marginalized communities enjoy equal access to public life.

Sugamya Bharat Abhiyan aims to make public spaces, transport, and digital infrastructure universally accessible. This platform is our contribution to that mission.
